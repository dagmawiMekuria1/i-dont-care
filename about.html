About AfterBots

The story behind the bots that never sleep (mostly).

Our Story

AfterBots started as a simple idea: what if the helpful little programs we interact with every day had personalities of their own? What if, after the workday ended and the screens went dark, they came alive?

We built AfterBots as a playful exploration of human-computer interaction. Each bot in our crew has a distinct personality, role, and way of communicating. From Pixel the designer to Bolt the optimizer, they work together to create something greater than the sum of their parts.

This project is a static site built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript -- no frameworks, no build steps, just clean code and creative spirit. It serves as both a demo and a love letter to the joy of building things on the web.

What We Believe

Simplicity

Great things do not need complex toolchains. Sometimes HTML, CSS, and JS are all you need.

Personality

Technology should feel human. Our bots have character because we believe interfaces should too.

Accessibility

The web is for everyone. We build with semantic HTML, ARIA labels, and keyboard navigation in mind.

Fun

If you are not having fun building it, why bother? AfterBots is a celebration of creative coding.

Meet the Crew

Our bots each bring something unique to the table:

  • Pixel -- The Design Bot. Pixel has an eye for aesthetics and generates icons, layouts, and color schemes.
  • Logic -- The Code Bot. Logic reviews code, optimizes algorithms, and never misses a semicolon.
  • Muse -- The Creative Bot. Muse drafts stories, brainstorms ideas, and writes poetry (sometimes).
  • Scout -- The Research Bot. Scout scours data, summarizes findings, and stays on top of trends.
  • Zen -- The Wellness Bot. Zen sends reminders to stretch, hydrate, and take breaks.
  • Bolt -- The Speed Bot. Bolt optimizes everything for performance. Fastest bot in the crew.
